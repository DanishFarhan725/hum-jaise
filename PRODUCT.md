# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) deployed to Netlify. Chosen as the recommended option by the user: static today, with content collections and i18n routing so it can grow into pseudonymous accounts, discussions and moderation without a rewrite. Fonts self-hosted. No analytics, cookies, or third-party scripts.

## Users

Neurodivergent people in Pakistan: those with a diagnosis, those questioning or self-identifying, and people who simply relate to these experiences. Many have felt misunderstood, rejected, or "too much" in family, school, work and religious or social settings. They arrive cautious, often tired of explaining themselves, sometimes on a phone with limited data, sometimes reading in English as a second language.

## Product Purpose

Hum Jaise (ہم جیسے, "people like us") is a welcoming peer space that makes people feel seen, accepted and less alone. Today it is a small static site: shared experiences, resources, about, community principles, and an honest invitation to take part. Success is a visitor recognising themselves and feeling they do not have to pretend, explain, or change to belong.

## Positioning

Rooted in Pakistan's own languages and everyday life (family gatherings, school, rishta conversations, being called "nakhray"), not imported wellness language. Peer support, not clinical service. Belonging without proof of diagnosis.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site. No accounts, discussions, or moderation yet; these are planned and must be described as plans, never implied as existing.
- One optional participation form (Netlify Forms): a message plus an optional way to reply. No name, phone, or diagnosis requested. Honeypot spam protection. Plain-language privacy page.
- Reading-comfort controls (text size, spacing, motion, theme), stored only on the visitor's device.
- English primary; Urdu (Nastaliq, RTL) for hero, principles and key lines; structured for a full Urdu locale later.
- Not a medical or crisis service. No medical claims, no invented statistics, no fabricated testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hum Jaise / ہم جیسے.
- Hero line: "Come in as you are. Nobody here needs you to be easier to understand."
- Colours soft, welcoming and loving; not bright or bold. Type highly readable and easy on the eyes.
- Avoid the puzzle-piece symbol. Respect self-chosen identity language; the site itself says "neurodivergent people".

## Evidence on Hand

- No real member stories yet. Experiences are written as recognisable prompts, clearly not quotes from real people.
- No vetted resource list from the owner; researched entries are marked "verify before launch".
- About: run by a small anonymous peer group. Contact email not yet provided (placeholder).

## Product Principles

1. You don't have to prove you belong.
2. Listen before you try to fix.
3. Let people define themselves.
4. Protect each other's privacy, without promising absolute anonymity.
5. Make room for different ways of being.
6. Disagree without demeaning.
7. Share experiences, not medical instructions.
8. Support without judgment or pressure.

These are the community's intended principles; any future platform's moderation rules derive from them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect reduced motion. Low sensory load: no autoplay, no flashing, calm motion only. Inclusive of Pakistan's diverse backgrounds, languages and beliefs without assuming shared faith or experience.
