# Home (and shared site shell)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a cautious neurodivergent person in Pakistan, often on a phone, tired of explaining. Job: recognise themselves, feel welcome, then read experiences or take part. Content: hero line (pinned), experience prompts (authored, labelled as not real quotes), 8 principles (user-supplied), honest invitation. No fabricated stories, stats, or features.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a sehan, the open courtyard of an old house where anyone may sit down. Refuses the category default of pastel blobs, stock smiling faces and a centred slogan over three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Lime-washed plaster in pale dusk lilac, aubergine ink, neem-leaf sage for actions, apricot sunlight. One jaali lattice geometry (eight-point star grid) drives the falling light, section thresholds and the focus ring alike. Arched thresholds; no cards. Urdu in Gulzar Nastaliq, English display in Young Serif, body in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.

STORY: You are welcome as you are -> this might sound like you -> this is how we will treat each other -> come in, quietly, if and when you want.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Soft jaali light falls across the whole plaster wall at the angle of the visitor's local hour. Headline large, left aligned, set inside the light; Urdu line beneath right-to-left. One sage primary action "Read experiences like yours", one quiet link "How to take part". Plain nav top.
